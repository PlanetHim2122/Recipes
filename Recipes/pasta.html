Alfredo Pasta Recipe

Back to HomePage

Description

This is a simple and easy to make Alfredo Pasta Recipe. It is a classic Italian dish that is perfect for a quick and easy meal.

Ingredients list

  • Pasta
  • Alfredo Sauce
  • Parmesan Cheese
  • Butter
  • Milk
  • Salt
  • Pepper

Steps

  1. Boil the pasta
  2. Add the Alfredo Sauce to the pasta
  3. Add the Parmesan Cheese to the pasta
  4. Add the Butter to the pasta
  5. Add the Milk to the pasta
  6. Add the Salt to the pasta
  7. Add the Pepper to the pasta

Cooking time

20 minutes

Servings

4 servings

Nutritional information

Calories: 1000

Protein: 90g

Carbohydrates: 80g

Fat: 20g
