Description

This is a simple and easy to make a Burger.

Ingredients list

  • Burger Patty
  • Secret Sauce
  • American Cheese
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato
  • Pickles
  • Onions

Steps

  1. Toast the Buns
  2. Add the Spread to both sides
  3. Add the ingredients on one bun
  4. add spread to other bun and put on top
